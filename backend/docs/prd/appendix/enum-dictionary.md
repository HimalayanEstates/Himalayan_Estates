# Enum Dictionary

## Property category

`hotel`, `land`, `house_villa`, `apartment`, `commercial`, `resort`, `farm`, `other`.

## Purpose

`sale`, `lease`, `rent`, `sale_or_lease`.

## Listing status

`draft` — editable and private; `in_review` — awaiting approval; `published` — public and searchable; `paused` — temporarily hidden; `archived` — no longer active.

## Pricing mode

`fixed`, `negotiable`, `price_on_request`, `range`.

## Inquiry status

`new`, `contacted`, `qualified`, `visit_scheduled`, `won`, `lost`, `spam`.

## Staff role

`broker`, `agent`, `administrator`.

## Verification

`unverified`, `pending`, `verified`, `rejected`.

Category-specific fields are conditional, not separate categories: hotel/resort may include rooms, occupancy, and operating status; land may include area, zoning, frontage, and title-document availability; residential property may include bedrooms, bathrooms, furnishing, and parking; commercial property may include floor area, frontage, and permitted use.
